The Hidden Threat

One squirrel can cause thousands in damage in minutes.

Texas critters are attracted to the warmth and shelter created under solar panels. Without protection, squirrels and rodents chew through expensive DC wiring, creating fire hazards and system shutdowns.

  • Chewed DC wiring — most common cause of fire hazards in residential solar
  • Nesting materials blocking ventilation — causes overheating and reduced output
  • Pest damage often voids manufacturer warranties — protect your coverage
  • Average repair cost without protection: $1,500–$3,000 per incident

What We Inspect

Every component. Every connection. Every concern.

Regular inspections catch problems before they become costly failures. Our certified technicians evaluate your entire system — from panel surface to utility interconnect — and deliver a detailed written report with photos and prioritized recommendations.

  • Panel condition and physical damage — cracks, delamination, soiling, and discoloration
  • Mounting system integrity — racking, fasteners, flashing, and roof penetration seals
  • Wiring and electrical connections — corrosion, arc faults, and conductor integrity
  • Inverter performance and error codes — efficiency, clipping, and fault history
  • Energy production levels — actual vs. expected output with weather normalization
  • Grounding system verification — NEC compliance and safety continuity
  • Shade analysis and obstructions — new tree growth, HVAC additions, or neighbor structures
  • System safety compliance — all components verified against current code requirements
Inspection Benefits

Why annual inspections pay for themselves.

🔬

Inspection Process

Thorough, systematic, and professional.

01

Visual Inspection

Complete examination of panels, mounting hardware, conduit runs, and all visible system components from ground and rooftop vantage points.

02

Electrical Testing

Test all electrical connections, grounding continuity, bonding, and safety disconnects against NEC and manufacturer specifications.

03

Performance Analysis

Measure actual output versus expected production using weather-normalized benchmarks from your Know TrueUp® production history.

04

Inverter Diagnostics

Check inverter operation, error codes, clipping events, and communication to confirm full system efficiency.

05

Thermal Imaging

Infrared scan to identify hot spots, failing cells, connection issues, and potential fire hazards invisible to the naked eye.

06

Report Generation

Comprehensive documentation with photos, test results, and a prioritized recommendation list delivered the same day.
